# Vibe Check: Understanding the Effects of LLM-Based Conversational Agents&#39; Personality and Alignment on User Perceptions in Goal-Oriented Tasks

- **Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.09870
- **Date**: 2025-09-11
- **Authors / Org**: Hasibur Rahman, Smit Desai
- **Type**: arxiv

## Core Problem

While LLMs enable conversational agents to express distinct personalities, it is unclear how varying levels of personality expression and the alignment between user and agent personality influence user perceptions in goal-oriented tasks, a gap this study addresses.

## Core Approach

1. **Trait Modulation Keys framework**: A novel method to control the expression level of Big Five personality traits in LLM-based agents, enabling low, medium, or high expression conditions.

2. **Between-subjects experiment**: Participants (N=150) completed a travel planning task with agents exhibiting one of three expression levels, allowing causal inference on perception differences.

3. **Personality alignment measurement**: User and agent personality traits were measured and compared to assess how alignment (similarity) affects perceptions, using cluster analysis to identify compatibility profiles.

## Evidence

Medium expression significantly outperformed low and high extremes across Intelligence, Enjoyment, Anthropomorphism, Intention to Adopt, Trust, and Likeability. Extraversion and Emotional Stability were the most influential traits for alignment. Cluster analysis identified three profiles, with 'Well-Aligned' users reporting substantially positive perceptions.

## Assumptions & Open Questions

- ⚠️ The study assumes that the Big Five traits are the most relevant dimensions for CA personality, but other traits or cultural factors may be equally or more important.
- ⚠️ The experiment uses a single goal-oriented task (travel planning), limiting generalizability to other domains like healthcare or education.
- ⚠️ The 'medium expression' level is defined by the Trait Modulation Keys framework, but its calibration may not be universally optimal across different LLMs or contexts.
- ⚠️ The study does not control for potential confounding effects of user prior experience with LLMs or conversational agents.
- ⚠️ The long-term effects of personality expression and alignment on user trust and adoption are not examined, only immediate perceptions.
